Team Working Agreement Goal
Goal -To collaboratively deliver a working Northstar Support MVP that addresses the identified customer support problems and produces clear evidence of our team’s work.
Rules of work
Every task must have a clear owner
Tasks must be small enough to complete and verify
Team members communicate progress regularly through WhatsApp and through Meet
Blockers are reported as soon as they occur
Work is tracked on the team’s board
Team members review important work before final submission
Communication Agreement
Communication channels - Google Meet and WhatsApp
Team Members to respond to important project messages within an agreed reasonable period and communicate blockers rather than going silent
 Decision Making and Conflict Process.
Members will raise their concerns and discuss them with each other. If not solved, the members involved will discuss their issues with the team. And the project requirements and agreed goals will be used to guide the final decisions. If an agreement is not reached, the Team Lead will then facilitate the discussion and help the team reach a decision.
Important decisions will be documented so that the team has a shared record.
